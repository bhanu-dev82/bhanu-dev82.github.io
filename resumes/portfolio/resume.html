02 THE SYNC ENGINE

Zero data loss. Even at zero bars.

This is the sync architecture that powers PyMaster — rebuilt live, right here. Events are captured into a persistent local queue, batched, and flushed to Firestore on reconnect.

  • Persistent queue — every event hits disk (Hive) before anything else. Kill the app, kill the network: state survives.
  • Batched flush — on reconnect, events upload in batches with exponential backoff. Fewer writes, lower cost, same truth.
  • Deterministic conflicts — last-write-wins per field, resolved locally. No spinners, no lost progress, ever.
